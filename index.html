Quick Rules Reference
• Dealer Qualifies with Queen-high or better.
• Win (+2x): Dealer qualifies & player wins (Ante 1:1, Play 1:1).
• DNQ (+1x): Dealer does not qualify (Ante pays 1:1, Play pushes).
• Fold (-1x): Player forfeits Ante only.
• Lose (-2x): Dealer qualifies & beats player.
• Bonus: Straight (+1x), Trio (+3x), Straight Flush (+4x), Mini Royale (+5x).
